CAPTAIN VIKRAM BATRA

Birth Date

September 9, 1974

Death Date

July 7, 1999

About Vikram Batra

Captain Vikram Batra was an esteemed officer of the Indian Army, born on September 9, 1974, in Palampur, Himachal Pradesh. He was a brilliant student and a dedicated athlete, known for his leadership qualities from a young age. Batra joined the Indian Military Academy and was commissioned into the 13th Battalion, Jammu and Kashmir Rifles.

His exemplary service during the Kargil War in 1999 marked him as a national hero. Batra's unwavering courage and determination were evident during some of the fiercest battles, where he led his troops to victory against all odds.

Achievements

  • Awarded the Param Vir Chakra for his valor and sacrifice.
  • He successfully led operations to capture crucial points like Point 5140 and Point 4875.
  • He is recognized as a symbol of courage, inspiring countless individuals in the nation.
  • His charismatic personality and catchphrase, "Yeh Dil Maange More!" became very famous during the war.

Famous Quotes

"Either I will come back after hoisting the flag, or I will come back wrapped in it."

"I want to live for my country. I want to die for my country."

Legacy

Captain Vikram Batra's legacy continues to inspire generations. His bravery and commitment to the nation have made him a revered figure in India. Schools, colleges, and institutions honor his memory by organizing events and programs that promote patriotism and valor.

His story has been depicted in films, documentaries, and literature, ensuring that the spirit of his sacrifice is never forgotten. The Indian Army, along with citizens across the country, remember him as a hero who laid down his life for the motherland.
