CAPTAIN VIKRAM BATRA

Param Vir Chakra (Posthumous) | 13 JAK Rifles

Birth Date

September 9, 1974 (Palampur, Himachal Pradesh)

Martyrdom Date

July 7, 1999 (Point 4875, Kargil)

About Vikram Batra

Captain Vikram Batra was an esteemed officer of the Indian Army, born on September 9, 1974, in Palampur, Himachal Pradesh. He was a brilliant student and a dedicated athlete, known for his leadership qualities from a young age. Batra joined the Indian Military Academy and was commissioned into the 13th Battalion, Jammu and Kashmir Rifles.

His exemplary service during the Kargil War in 1999 marked him as a national hero. Batra's unwavering courage and determination were evident during some of the fiercest battles, where he led his troops to victory against all odds.

Achievements

  • • Awarded the Param Vir Chakra, India's highest military honor, for his valor and sacrifice.
  • • Successfully led operations to capture crucial peaks, including Point 5140 and Point 4875.
  • • Recognized across the nation as an enduring symbol of selfless courage and leadership.
  • • His inspiring battle cry, "Yeh Dil Maange More!", rallied his troops and resonated nationwide.

Famous Quotes

"Either I will come back after hoisting the flag, or I will come back wrapped in it."

"I want to live for my country. I want to die for my country."

Legacy

Captain Vikram Batra's legacy continues to inspire generations of soldiers and civilians alike. Schools, colleges, and national institutions honor his memory with awards and programs promoting patriotism and courage.

His story has been celebrated in books, documentaries, and the film Shershaah, ensuring that the spirit of his supreme sacrifice remains immortal in the heart of the nation.
